## What Enko is

Enko is a crop-health R&D company, not a software vendor. Its product is chemistry —
weed control, disease control, insect control and soil-pest control compounds — advanced
through partnerships with agrochemical companies rather than sold as a platform.

- ENKOMPASS combines DNA-encoded libraries, AI/ML and structure-based design to screen
  billions of molecules for new modes of action and lead compounds.
- Named partners and backers include Syngenta, Bayer, Nufarm and the Gates Foundation
  Strategic Investment Fund.
- Campus: 89,000 sq ft of research labs and greenhouses on 15.63 acres in Mystic, CT.
